    Syntactic statistics¶

    SyntaxStats(source)

    The syntactic statistics of a text by its dependency tree (Ivanov, Solnyshkina and Solovyev 2018): the measures of the tree and of the syntactic constructions. The data source is a Doc object of spaCy with a dependency parse and parts of speech. Punctuation, symbols and whitespace are no nodes of the tree, and the distances are counted in positions of words. The units of L2SCA, the prepositional chains and the similarity of sentences are computed on first use, so a subclass whose stats_desc does not name them does not pay for them.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a source that is not a Doc;
    • SourceError - a source without a dependency parse (relations and sentence boundaries), without parts of speech, without lemmas when the class requires them, or without words.

    The class extends the SyntaxStats of the anyTS core with the Russian layer. The relations and features are those of the Universal Dependencies annotation of the ru_core_news_sm, ru_core_news_md and ru_core_news_lg models; the quality of the statistics is bounded by the quality of the parse: the small ru_core_news_sm model errs in parts of speech and relations noticeably more often than ru_core_news_lg. The parts of a hyphenated word that spaCy splits are joined (во-первых is one word), and the clauses, participial clauses, negations and split predicates follow the rules of Russian given in the cards of the metrics. ruTS adds its own metrics of genitive chains and adverbial participle clauses. The syntactic markers of officialese are the passive, participial clauses, genitive chains, split predicates and the ratio of nouns to verbs. The print_stats table and the error messages are in Russian (SYNTAX_STATS_DESC and CORE_MESSAGES of ruts.constants).

    Ivanov, Solnyshkina and Solovyev tested the features on a corpus of school textbooks: the numbers of coordination chains, participial clauses and noun phrase modifiers correlate with the textbook grade at 0.88-0.93. The cards of the metrics name the original features of the paper and their correlation coefficients with the textbook grade.

    import spacy
from ruts import SyntaxStats

nlp = spacy.load("ru_core_news_sm")
text = "Дом, построенный рабочими в прошлом году, был продан. Он сказал, что не придёт, и ушёл, хлопнув дверью."
ss = SyntaxStats(nlp(text))
ss.print_stats()

    Metrics¶

    Every metric is an attribute of the object, computed when it is created - the units of L2SCA, the prepositional chains and the similarity of sentences on first use - and get_stats gives them all. The measures of a sentence (the longest dependency, the depth, the nodes per leaf) are averaged over the sentences; the constructions are given per sentence.

    mean_dependency_distance Mean distance between a word and its head 1 and above, nan without dependencies Harder to process

    max_dependency_distance Mean of the longest dependencies of the sentences that have dependencies 1 and above, nan without dependencies Longer longest dependencies
    p_adjacent_dependencies Share of the dependencies of length 1 0-1, nan without dependencies More links between neighbours
    tree_depth Mean depth of the tree of a sentence 0 and above Deeper embedding

    Dependency distance¶

    mean_dependency_distance · std_dependency_distance · max_dependency_distance · p_adjacent_dependencies

    calc_dependency_distances(tokens, join_hyphens=False)

    The dependency distances (Liu 2008): the distance between a word and its head in positions of words, punctuation left out, in the order of the words. The head of a sentence and a word whose head lies outside the sequence are skipped.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc / Span / list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one position

    The mean dependency distance grows with sentence length, and the longest one is the best of the tree features at the level of a single sentence. One-word sentences enter none of the metrics of the distances.

    import spacy
from ruts.syntax_stats import calc_dependency_distances

nlp = spacy.load("ru_core_news_sm")
doc = nlp("Повышение эффективности использования ресурсов предприятия обсуждалось на совещании.")
calc_dependency_distances(doc)

    [5, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2]

    Tree depth¶

    tree_depth

    calc_tree_depth(tokens, join_hyphens=False)

    The depth of the dependency tree: the longest path from the head of a sentence to a leaf, in relations between words; the maximum for several sentences, 0 for a sentence of one word. With join_hyphens a hyphenated word is one word, and the words that hang on any of its parts are one level below it.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc / Span / list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    The depth is LONGEST_PATH of the paper of Ivanov et al. (r = 0.84).

    Leaves and subtrees¶

    leaves_per_sent · subtrees_per_sent · nodes_per_leaf

    A leaf is a word without dependent words (count_children), a subtree a word with them. The nodes per leaf are the words of a sentence over its leaves, averaged over the \(S\) sentences.

    \[ \frac{1}{S} \sum_{s=1}^{S} \frac{\textrm{Number of words of } s}{\textrm{Number of leaves of } s} \]

    The features of the paper: LEAVES_NUMBER (r = 0.84), PATH_NUMBER (r = 0.87) and AVERAGE_PATH (r = 0.84). The distribution of the words by number of dependents, the branching profile, is the attribute c_children.

    Words before the root¶

    words_before_root · Coh-Metrix

    The left embeddedness of Coh-Metrix (SYNLE): the number of the words of a sentence before its root, the main predicate, averaged over the sentences whose root is a word. The root of a sentence with a copula is its nominal part (she is a doctor), as Universal Dependencies has it. In a language with a free word order the measure reads the order as much as the complexity.

    Valency of the finite verbs¶

    verb_valency

    calc_valency(token, join_hyphens=False)

    The valency of a token: the number of its dependent words, the relations cc, conj, parataxis and punct left out (anyts.constants.VALENCY_IGNORED_DEPS); with join_hyphens=True a hyphenated dependent counts once, so came in Some-one came has one dependent and not two.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    A finite verb is one of is_finite_verb; the valency is VERBS_DEP of the paper (r = 0.43).

    Coordination chains¶

    coordination_chains_per_sent · mean_coordination_chain_len

    calc_coordination_chains(tokens, join_hyphens=False)

    The lengths of the coordination chains, in the order of their first words. A chain is a group of words linked by conj, whichever word each conjunct hangs on: Universal Dependencies attaches every conjunct to the first one (pears → apples, plums → apples), ClearNLP to the previous one (pears → apples, plums → pears); both give one chain of three for apples, pears and plums. A nested coordination (cats and dogs, or birds) and an enumeration split between several heads are one chain too. The length of a chain is the number of its words; a conjunct whose head lies outside the sequence is not linked to it. With join_hyphens a hyphenated word is one conjunct, linked by the conj of any of its parts (member and vice-governor is a chain of two).

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc / Span / list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    The number of chains per sentence (SOCHIN_NUMBER, r = 0.93) and their mean length (AVERAGE_SOCHIN_LENGTH, r = 0.87) are the strongest syntactic predictors of textbook complexity.

    Clauses¶

    clauses_per_sent · mean_clause_len

    is_clause_head(token)

    Checks whether a word heads a clause: the head of the sentence or a word with ccomp, advcl, acl, csubj, parataxis and their subtypes (anyts.constants.CLAUSE_DEPS), except full participles, adverbial participles and infinitives under a noun («желание уйти»): participial and adverbial participle clauses are counted apart, and a short participle («дом построен») is the predicate of its clause. Under xcomp only an infinitive of is_infinitive_clause heads a clause: a finite form under xcomp is a parse error of the Russian models. An infinitive subject (csubj) of a modal word («нужно уйти», «можно войти», «следует отметить») heads no clause of its own. A parenthetical construction (parataxis) and a coordinated predicate (a conj relation from the clause head) form their own clause only if they can be a predicate (is_predicate): «Он сказал: „Уходи“» and «думала, что он умен» are clauses, while the parentheticals «например», «конечно», «во-первых», which spaCy models also attach as parataxis, are not.

    is_infinitive_clause(token)

    Checks whether an infinitive under xcomp heads a clause of its own («хочет уйти», «попросил его уйти»): it does not when it makes one predicate with its head (is_infinitive_periphrasis) or stands in a participial or adverbial participle clause («решивший уйти», «желая уйти»).

    is_infinitive_periphrasis(token)

    Checks whether an infinitive makes one predicate with its head: a modal or phasal verb or a modal word of INFINITIVE_PERIPHRASES («может уйти», «должен прийти», «начал читать», «взялся читать», «перестал курить», «пришлось уйти», «стоит отметить», «нужно уйти», «можно войти», «пора идти») or the hortative «давай», «давайте» of HORTATIVE_WORDS without a negation («давайте играть», but not «не давайте детям играть»).

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token

    Subordinate clauses¶

    subordinate_clauses_per_sent · p_complex_sents

    is_subordinate_clause_head(token)

    Checks whether a word heads a subordinate clause: a clause head (is_clause_head) with ccomp, advcl, acl, csubj and their subtypes, or a coordinated predicate of a subordinate clause.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token

    The features of the paper: PODCHIN_RATE (r = 0.64), the subordinate clauses per sentence, and PODCHIN_NUMBER (r = 0.62), the share of the sentences with at least one subordinate clause.

    Infinitive clauses¶

    infinitive_clauses_per_sent

    is_infinitive_clause_head(token)

    Checks whether a word heads an infinitive clause under a verb: an infinitive under xcomp that heads a clause of its own (is_infinitive_clause: «хочет уйти», «попросил его уйти»), or an infinitive coordinated with it. Such a clause is counted apart from the subordinate ones, as grammars of many languages take it for a part of a simple sentence.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token 
    import spacy
from ruts.syntax_stats import is_clause_head, is_infinitive_clause_head, is_subordinate_clause_head

nlp = spacy.load("ru_core_news_sm")
doc = nlp("Он хочет уйти, но может остаться, если его попросят.")
[token.text for token in doc if is_clause_head(token)]
[token.text for token in doc if is_subordinate_clause_head(token)]
[token.text for token in doc if is_infinitive_clause_head(token)]

    Production units of L2SCA¶

    t_units_per_sent · mean_t_unit_len · clauses_per_t_unit · p_complex_t_units · verb_phrases_per_t_unit · coordinate_phrases_per_clause · complex_nominals_per_clause · Lu 2010

    The indices of the L2 Syntactic Complexity Analyzer (L2SCA) that the other metrics do not give, on Universal Dependencies. MLC and C/S are mean_clause_len and clauses_per_sent; MLS and DC/C come from the counts, n_words / n_sents and n_subordinate_clauses / n_clauses.

    • A T-unit is a main clause with the clauses that depend on it: the head of a sentence and every clause head joined to it through conj or parataxis (anyts.constants.MAIN_CLAUSE_DEPS), so I came, and he left holds two; a conjunct of a subordinate clause is none. A conjunct without a subject of its own (I came and left) is a predicate sharing the T-unit of the clause it is coordinated to, as in L2SCA, unless its person, number or gender - those of its auxiliary or copula, its own without one - differ from those of that clause: in a language that drops its subjects the ending names another one (llegamos al puerto y nos esperabas). An existential there (expl of ClearNLP) is a subject. A T-unit is complex with a subordinate clause (is_subordinate_clause_head) whose nearest T-unit above is it.
    • A verb phrase is a predicate (is_predicate) that is no auxiliary or copula: a verb, an auxiliary used as a predicate (there is trouble), a word with a subject or a copula.
    • A coordinate phrase is a coordination with a conjunct that is no clause head (the man and the woman, is big and nice) or a predicate without a subject of its own (came and left), counted once by its first conjunct; a coordination of clauses with their own subjects counts as clauses.
    • The clauses are those of is_clause_head, as in the other metrics: a predicate sharing a subject and an infinitive clause count as clauses, while L2SCA counts finite clauses with a subject, so C/T, C/S and CN/C run higher and MLC lower than in L2SCA.
    • A complex nominal is a noun with a modifier of anyts.constants.COMPLEX_NOMINAL_DEPS (an adjective, a complement, a relative or participial clause, an apposition, a possessive), or a nominal clause, a clause head of anyts.constants.NOMINAL_CLAUSE_DEPS or a conjunct of one.
    \[ \textrm{MLT} = \frac{\textrm{Words}}{\textrm{T-units}}, \quad \textrm{C/T} = \frac{\textrm{Clauses}}{\textrm{T-units}}, \quad \textrm{CT/T} = \frac{\textrm{Complex T-units}}{\textrm{T-units}}, \quad \textrm{CN/C} = \frac{\textrm{Complex nominals}}{\textrm{Clauses}} \]

    Modifiers of a noun¶

    modifiers_per_noun

    count_noun_modifiers(token, join_hyphens=False)

    Prepositional chains¶

    prep_chains_per_sent · max_prep_chain_len · Profiling-UD

    A prepositional chain is two or more nested complements with a preposition, each hanging on the previous one: on the mat of the house of the mayor (length 3). The links are the words of the hook is_prep_complement, and the chains and their lengths are those of calc_modifier_chains; a chain may start at a complement of a verb (obl) or at a prepositional predicate with a copula (was in the house of the mayor), but not at a conjunct of a complement (in Madrid and in the centre of the city), and a predicate of a relative clause (the house that was by the river) is no link of the chain of its noun. The chains read Universal Dependencies alone: ClearNLP heads a phrase by its preposition (prep, pobj), so its parses give no chains.

    The genitive chains of ruTS (genitive_chains_per_sent) are counted apart: their links are prepositionless genitive modifiers («повышение эффективности использования ресурсов»), and those of a prepositional chain are complements with a preposition.

    Order of the subjects and the objects¶

    p_preverbal_subjects · p_preverbal_objects · Profiling-UD

    The order of the subjects and of the direct objects in the manner of Profiling-UD: the shares of those before their head, the predicate. Only the nominal ones (NOUN, PROPN) count: the place of a pronoun, such as a clitic object, is set by the grammar. A subject is a word of nsubj (anyts.constants.NOMINAL_SUBJECT_DEPS), an object one of obj (anyts.constants.OBJECT_DEPS), with the relations of ClearNLP and the subtypes.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Nominal subjects before their head}}{\textrm{Nominal subjects}}, \quad \frac{\textrm{Nominal objects before their head}}{\textrm{Nominal objects}} \]

    Similarity of adjacent sentences¶

    pos_edit_distance · syntax_similarity · Coh-Metrix

    How alike the structures of two adjacent sentences are, averaged over the \(S - 1\) pairs. pos_edit_distance is the edit distance of the parts of speech \(P_s\) of the words of the sentences (SYNMEDpos of Coh-Metrix) over the length \(n\) of the longer sentence of the pair, calc_edit_distance. syntax_similarity is the similarity of the multisets of the dependency triples \(T\) of the two sentences (dependency_triples, calc_multiset_similarity), in the manner of SYNSTRUT of Coh-Metrix, which compares the nodes of phrase-structure trees.

    \[ \frac{1}{S - 1} \sum_{s=1}^{S-1} \frac{\textrm{lev}(P_s, P_{s+1})}{\max(n_s, n_{s+1})}, \quad \frac{1}{S - 1} \sum_{s=1}^{S-1} \frac{|T_s \cap T_{s+1}|}{|T_s \cup T_{s+1}|} \]

    Ratio of the nouns to the verbs¶

    noun_verb_ratio

    The number of the nouns n_nouns to the number of the verb forms n_verbs.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of nouns}}{\textrm{Number of verb forms}} \]

    The words of a sequence of tokens in their order; with join_hyphens=True a word the tokenizer split at its hyphens is one word, given by its part that holds its relation, as with the dependents of get_children (governor in He is vice-governor), or by its first part when a hyphen holds it.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc / Span / list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    Hyphenated words¶

    A tokenizer may split a word at its hyphens (well-known into well, -, known), and a parser then hangs every part on its own head. With join_hyphens=True the functions of the tree take a word whose parts anyts.utils.joins_previous joins for one word with its hyphens. A word holding the head of the sentence has no head; the head of another one is that of its part hanging outside it, the nearest to the root (a word rather than a hyphen when equal). The dependents of a word are the words whose head hangs on one of its tokens, each given by that part (or by its first part when it hangs by a hyphen); its subtree is the word and the words that hang on it, directly or through punctuation. So every word has one dependency and is the dependent of one word.

    SyntaxStats and the functions of the Russian constructions always join hyphenated words. The functions of the core, is_predicate, count_noun_modifiers, is_passive, has_agent and is_agentless included, take join_hyphens=False by default: they give the values of SyntaxStats with join_hyphens=True.

    Chains of modifiers¶

    Edit distance and multiset similarity¶

    calc_edit_distance(sents)

    The mean edit distance of the items of adjacent sentences: the Levenshtein distance of every two - the fewest insertions, deletions and substitutions that turn one into the other - over the number of the items of the longer one, 0 for the same sequences and 1 for ones with no item in common; nan for fewer than two sentences.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    sents list[list] - Items of every sentence, such as its parts of speech

    Sentences that are not a list of lists, or items that cannot be hashed, raise SourceTypeError.

    calc_multiset_similarity(sents)

    The mean similarity of the multisets of the items of adjacent sentences: the weighted Jaccard similarity of every two - the items they share, each as many times as the sentence with fewer of them has it, over the items of both, each as many times as the sentence with more of them has it - 1 for the same multisets and for two empty ones, 0 for disjoint ones; nan for fewer than two sentences. A mapping is read by its keys, as a set.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    sents list[list] - Items of every sentence, such as its dependency triples

    Sentences that are not a list of collections, or items that cannot be hashed, raise SourceTypeError.
