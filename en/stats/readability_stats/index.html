    Readability metrics¶

    ReadabilityStats(source, sents_extractor=None, words_extractor=None, preset='plainrussian')

    The readability metrics of a text from its basic statistics: the mean sentence length in words, the mean word length in syllables or letters, the share of polysyllabic and of long words and the variability of word length. The data source is a text, a Doc object of spaCy or ready basic statistics, which are reused as they are; the extractors are passed on to the basic statistics of a text or a Doc. The formulas count only the letters of the counted words, so a word extractor that drops words does not lengthen the mean word.

    The metrics are properties computed on each access, so a change of the coefficients of an object applies at once.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a source that is neither a string, a Doc nor basic statistics, basic statistics of a class other than those of the library, an extractor of another type, a string or a Doc to a class without basic_stats_class;
    • SourceError - a source without words or sentences;
    • ParameterError - an unknown preset or one that is not a string.

    The metrics assess readability on the side of the language - the complexity of words and syntactic constructions, not the typographic design of the text. They presume that short sentences are easier to read than long ones, long words make reading harder, and low-frequency and unfamiliar words slow a reader down.

    The class extends the ReadabilityStats of the anyTS core with the hooks of Russian, given in the cards of the metrics: the basic statistics of ruTS, which take the Russian SentsExtractor and WordsExtractor by default, the coefficient presets, the Russian formulas, the consensus grade over the formulas of ruTS, the stages of the Russian school, the scales with Russian labels and the reading speeds of Russian readers. The print_stats table and the error messages are in Russian (READABILITY_STATS_DESC and CORE_MESSAGES of ruts.constants).

    from ruts import ReadabilityStats

text = "Ног нет, а хожу, рта нет, а скажу: когда спать, когда вставать, когда работу начинать"
rs = ReadabilityStats(text)
rs.print_stats()

    Parameters¶

    Parameter Type Default Description
    source str / Doc / BasicStats - Data source (a string, a Doc object or ready basic statistics)
    sents_extractor SentsExtractor None Sentence extraction tool
    words_extractor WordsExtractor None Word extraction tool
    preset str 'plainrussian' Coefficient preset: plainrussian, fiction or academic

    The object keeps the basic statistics of the text in bs, the name of the preset in preset, read-only, and the coefficients of the preset by formula in coefficients, a copy that can be changed for one object. An object of another preset can reuse the basic statistics: ReadabilityStats(rs.bs, preset="fiction").

    Coefficient presets¶

    The coefficients of the formulas are parameters of their functions, and a preset gives them by formula. The presets differ in the coefficients of the Flesch-Kincaid test and the Flesch index; the coefficients of the Coleman-Liau, SMOG and ARI indices in all presets come from the plainrussian project, since no other adaptations for Russian have been published, and the other formulas do not depend on the preset.

    Preset Source Corpus Flesch-Kincaid test Flesch index
    plainrussian Plain Russian Language (Begtin) 68 grade-labeled texts 0.318·ASL + 14.2·ASW − 30.5 206.835 − 1.3·ASL − 60.1·ASW (Oborneva, variant A, since plainrussian does not derive the Flesch index)
    fiction Oborneva (2005/2006) about 6 million words of fiction 0.5·ASL + 8.4·ASW − 15.59 206.835 − 1.3·ASL − 60.1·ASW (variant A)
    academic Solovyev, Ivanov, Solnyshkina (2018); Solnyshkina and Kiselnikov (2015) 14 social studies textbooks for grades 5-11 0.36·ASL + 5.76·ASW − 11.97 (FKG_SIS) 206.836 − 1.52·ASL − 65.14·ASW (Oborneva, variant B)

    Here ASL is the mean number of words per sentence, ASW the mean number of syllables per word. The table of all coefficients is ruts.constants.READABILITY_PRESETS.

On the TextsByGrade dataset the Spearman correlation of the Flesch-Kincaid test with the grade label for all three presets is 0.79, while the medians of the test by label group differ:

    On school texts (labels 1-11) the test with the fiction preset is about four grades above the label (median deviation +3.9 against −0.9 for plainrussian and −0.2 for academic), and about five on the first-grade reader SovChLit. Its value should therefore not be read as a grade of the Russian school: for the grade of the reader use plainrussian (the default; its coefficients were fitted on TextsByGrade) or academic, and fiction to compare with works that use Oborneva's coefficients. The consensus grade depends on the preset less, since the Flesch-Kincaid test is only one of its formulas (the median on TextsByGrade is 7.75 for fiction against 6.75 for plainrussian).

    Metrics¶

    A formula is computed by the function of its card on the counts of bs; the default coefficients of the functions are adapted for Russian. The core formulas take their coefficients from coefficients, where the preset sets those of the Flesch-Kincaid test, the Flesch index and the Coleman-Liau, SMOG and ARI indices, while the formulas of Solovyev, Ivanov and Solnyshkina, of Matskovsky and of Dale-Chall are computed with the coefficients of their functions, and coefficients does not affect them. A formula of counts gives nan when the number of words or of sentences it divides by is zero.

    Metric Measures Range Higher means
    flesch_kincaid_grade Grade by the sentence length and the syllables per word Years of schooling, below 0 for the simplest texts A harder text
    flesch_reading_easy Ease of reading by the sentence length and the syllables per word Nominally 0-100, above 100 for the simplest texts and below 0 for the hardest An easier text
    coleman_liau_index Grade by the letters and the sentences per 100 words Years of schooling, below 0 for the simplest texts A harder text
    smog_index Grade by the complex words per sentence Years of schooling A harder text
    automated_readability_index Grade by the letters per word and the sentence length Years of schooling, below 0 for the simplest texts A harder text
    lix Sentence length and the share of long words 0 and above A harder text
    rix Long words per sentence 0 and above A harder text
    sis_grade Grade by the sentence length and the letters per word School grade, below 0 for the simplest texts A harder text
    matskovsky_index Sentence length and the share of complex words 0 and above A harder text
    dale_chall_index Grade by the share of complex words and the sentence length Years of schooling A harder text
    gunning_fog_index Grade by the sentence length and the share of complex words Years of schooling A harder text

    reading_time Reading time at the reading speed (reading_speed) Minutes, above 0 A longer text

    Flesch-Kincaid grade¶

    flesch_kincaid_grade · Kincaid et al., 1975

    calc_flesch_kincaid_grade(n_syllables, n_words, n_sents, a=0.318, b=14.2, c=30.5)

    The years of schooling needed to read the text, by the mean sentence length in words and the mean word length in syllables.

    \[ a \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} + b \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of syllables}}{\textrm{Number of words}} - c \]

    The default coefficients come from the current version of the Plain Russian Language project. Other coefficients for Russian are given by the presets: Oborneva's (0.5, 8.4, 15.59) and FKG_SIS of Solovyev, Ivanov and Solnyshkina (0.36, 5.76, 11.97).

    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_syllables int - Number of syllables
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float 0.318 Coefficient a, at the mean sentence length
    b float 14.2 Coefficient b, at the mean word length
    c float 30.5 Coefficient c, the constant

    Flesch reading ease¶

    flesch_reading_easy · Flesch, 1948

    calc_flesch_reading_easy(n_syllables, n_words, n_sents, a=1.3, b=60.1, c=206.835)

    The ease of reading a text by the mean sentence length in words and the mean word length in syllables.

    \[ c - a \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} - b \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of syllables}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]

    The default coefficients are Oborneva's (2005/2006, variant A). Variant B of the same works (1.52, 65.14, 206.836), used by the Kazan group, is given by the academic preset.

    Parameter Type Default Description

    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float 1.3 Coefficient a, at the mean sentence length
    b float 60.1 Coefficient b, at the mean word length
    c float 206.835 Coefficient c, the constant

    The bands of describe are the scale ruts.constants.READING_EASE_LEVELS:

    Value Reading
    [90, ∞) 5th grade
    [80, 90) 6th grade
    [70, 80) 7th grade
    [60, 70) 8th and 9th grade
    [50, 60) 10th and 11th grade

    Coleman-Liau index¶

    coleman_liau_index · Coleman and Liau, 1975

    calc_coleman_liau_index(n_letters, n_words, n_sents, a=0.055, b=0.35, c=20.33)

    The years of schooling needed to read the text, by the letters and the sentences per 100 words.

    The default coefficients come from the Plain Russian Language project.

    SMOG index¶

    smog_index · McLaughlin, 1969

    calc_smog_index(n_complex, n_sents, a=1.1, b=64.6, c=0.05)

    The years of schooling needed to read the text, by the polysyllabic words per sentence. In the original formula the polysyllabic words have three or more syllables and b brings their number to a sample of 30 sentences.

    \[ a \cdot \sqrt{b \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of polysyllabic words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}}} + c \]

    A number of words or sentences that is not a non-negative integer raises ParameterError.

    The default coefficients of the Plain Russian Language project were fitted for complex words of five or more syllables, so ReadabilityStats passes the number of words of at least 5 syllables to the function (ruts.constants.SMOG_COMPLEX_SYL_FACTOR) rather than the n_complex_words attribute of the basic statistics.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_complex int - Number of complex words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float 1.1 Coefficient a, at the square root
    b float 64.6 Coefficient b, under the square root
    c float 0.05 Coefficient c, the constant

    LIX readability index¶

    lix · Björnsson, 1968

    calc_lix(n_long_words, n_words, n_sents)

    The mean sentence length plus the percentage of long words, the words of more than six letters; there are no coefficients to fit. The values read as text types:

    Value Text
    0-30 very easy texts, children's books
    30-40 easy texts, fiction, newspaper articles
    40-50 texts of medium difficulty, magazine articles
    50-60 hard texts, popular science, official texts
    60-100 very hard texts, laws and bureaucratic language
    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} + \frac{100 \cdot \textrm{Number of long words}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_long_words int - Number of long words
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences

    These bands are the scale of describe, ruts.constants.LIX_LEVELS; its last band is open above. The n_long_words attribute of the basic statistics of ruTS counts words of six or more letters, so ReadabilityStats passes the number of words of at least 7 letters to the function.

    Solovyev, Ivanov and Solnyshkina formula¶

    sis_grade · sis_grade_by_stage(stage) · Solovyev, Ivanov, Solnyshkina, 2023

    calc_sis_grade(n_letters, n_words, n_sents, a=-17.5, b=0.56, c=2.45)

    The school grade needed to read a Russian academic text, by the mean sentence length in words and the mean word length in letters, not in syllables as in the Flesch-Kincaid test. The formula was derived on the Russian Academic Corpus of 154 textbooks for grades 2-11 (5.7 million tokens); the mean error is about one grade. The authors computed the mean sentence length over spaCy tokens, which include punctuation, so the ruTS computation over words gives a slightly lower grade.

    \[ a + b \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} + c \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of letters}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_letters int - Number of letters of the words
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float -17.5 Coefficient a, the constant
    b float 0.56 Coefficient b, at the mean sentence length
    c float 2.45 Coefficient c, at the mean word length

    The default coefficients are those of the general formula. The coefficients the authors give for the education stages are in the ruts.constants.SIS_GRADE_STAGES table, taken by the sis_grade_by_stage method:

    Stage a b c
    grades 2-4 -2.59 0.17 0.61
    grades 5-7 -5.29 0.20 1.34
    grades 8-11 -3.26 0.21 1.35

    Solovyev, Ivanov and Solnyshkina formula with frequency¶

    sis_grade_by_freq(mean_ipm, stage) · Solovyev, Ivanov, Solnyshkina, 2023

    calc_sis_grade_freq(n_letters, n_words, n_sents, mean_ipm, a=-14.46, b=0.58, c=2.15, d=-0.0026)

    The variant of the general formula with a fourth feature FREQ2 - the mean frequency of the words of the text by the Lyashevskaya and Sharoff dictionary (table 7 of the paper): the more frequent the words, the lower the grade. In the paper FREQ2 values lie within 200-1000, which corresponds to the mean frequency of content words (LexicalStats.mean_ipm_content); the mean over all words including conjunctions and prepositions is several times larger. On the TextsByGrade dataset the formula with frequency gives the same correlation with the grade as the formula without it (Spearman's ρ 0.76 against 0.77), as in the paper.

    \[ a + b \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} + c \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of letters}}{\textrm{Number of words}} + d \cdot \textrm{FREQ2} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_letters int - Number of letters of the words
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    mean_ipm float - Mean frequency of content words (ipm)
    a float -14.46 Coefficient a, the constant
    b float 0.58 Coefficient b, at the mean sentence length
    c float 2.15 Coefficient c, at the mean word length
    d float -0.0026 Coefficient d, at the mean frequency

    The default coefficients are those of the general formula. The coefficients of the education stages from the same table are in the ruts.constants.SIS_GRADE_FREQ_STAGES table, taken by the stage parameter of the sis_grade_by_freq method:

    Stage a b c d
    grades 2-4 -1.21 0.2 0.56 -0.0025
    grades 5-7 -5.18 0.17 1.35 -0.00043
    grades 8-11 1.3 0.23 0.88 -0.0035

    Matskovsky formula¶

    matskovsky_index · Matskovsky, 1976

    calc_matskovsky_index(n_complex, n_words, n_sents, a=0.62, b=0.123, c=0.051)

    The first readability formula for Russian, derived by the method of successive intervals - by the mean sentence length and the percentage of complex words. A complex word here has more than three syllables, so ReadabilityStats passes the number of words of at least 4 syllables to the function.

    \[ a \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} + b \cdot \frac{100 \cdot \textrm{Number of complex words}}{\textrm{Number of words}} + c \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_complex int - Number of complex words
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float 0.62 Coefficient a, at the mean sentence length
    b float 0.123 Coefficient b, at the share of complex words
    c float 0.051 Coefficient c, the constant

    Dale-Chall index¶

    dale_chall_index · Dale and Chall, 1948

    calc_dale_chall_index(n_complex, n_words, n_sents, a=0.552, b=0.273)

    The years of schooling in the US system needed to understand the text, by the percentage of complex words and the mean sentence length. The original formula takes the share of unfamiliar words by a list of 3000 familiar ones; no such free list exists for Russian, so the adaptation of the Plain Russian Language project with the share of complex words is applied. A complex word has five or more syllables, so ReadabilityStats passes the number of words of at least 5 syllables to the function (ruts.constants.SMOG_COMPLEX_SYL_FACTOR). The original coefficients of the formula are 0.1579 and 0.0496.

    \[ a \cdot \frac{100 \cdot \textrm{Number of complex words}}{\textrm{Number of words}} + b \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_complex int - Number of complex words
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float 0.552 Coefficient a, at the share of complex words
    b float 0.273 Coefficient b, at the mean sentence length

    Gunning fog index¶

    gunning_fog_index · Gunning, 1952

    calc_gunning_fog_index(n_complex, n_words, n_sents, a=0.4)

    The years of schooling needed to read the text, by the mean sentence length and the percentage of complex words, in the original formula the words of three or more syllables.

    \[ a \cdot \left(\frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{Number of sentences}} + \frac{100 \cdot \textrm{Number of complex words}}{\textrm{Number of words}}\right) \]

    The index is used in the adaptation of the Plain Russian Language project: a complex word has five or more syllables, so ReadabilityStats passes the number of words of at least 5 syllables to the function (ruts.constants.SMOG_COMPLEX_SYL_FACTOR). SEO services additionally multiply the result by 0.78; no justification for this multiplier has been published.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_complex int - Number of complex words
    n_words int - Number of words
    n_sents int - Number of sentences
    a float 0.4 Coefficient a

    Consensus grade¶

    consensus_grade

    The consensus_grade attribute is the median of the values of the grade formulas (grade_stats) rounded half up, together with the reading ease in years of schooling (reading_ease_to_grade), not rounded. A library aligns the bounds of reading_ease_grades, and of each scale of preset_reading_ease_grades, with its scale of the reading ease, so that the consensus grade and describe read the value alike.

    The grade formulas of ruTS (ruts.constants.READABILITY_GRADE_STATS) are the Flesch-Kincaid test, the Coleman-Liau, SMOG, ARI, Dale-Chall and Gunning indices and the Solovyev, Ivanov and Solnyshkina formula, and the Flesch index is converted to a grade by the bands of its reading scale, so an index of 45 reads as university both in describe and in the consensus grade. The consensus grade is an analog of text_standard of textstat, which takes the mode instead of the median; the median is more robust to the outliers of individual formulas.

    calc_consensus_grade(grades, flesch_reading_easy=None, to_grade=flesch_reading_easy_to_grade)

    The median of the values of the grade formulas rounded half up, together with the reading ease in years of schooling by to_grade, not rounded - so a band of 8.5 votes for 8.5.

    Reading time¶

    reading_time · reading_time_by_speed(wpm) · reading_time_by_norm(norm) · Kuznetsov and Khromov, 1991

    calc_reading_time(n_words, wpm=180, *, messages=CORE_MESSAGES)

    The reading time of a text in minutes: the number of words divided by the reading speed.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of words}}{\textrm{wpm}} \]

    A number of words that is not a non-negative integer and a speed that is not a positive number raise ParameterError.

    The silent reading norm of an adult is 120-180 words per minute (Kuznetsov and Khromov, 1991); the upper bound is the default. The norms are in the ruts.constants.READING_SPEED_NORMS table, the reading-aloud norms of primary school by the Russian federal standard among them:

    Norm Words per minute
    adult_silent 120-180
    grade_1 25-40
    grade_2 60-80
    grade_3 80-100
    grade_4 90-110
    Parameter Type Default Description
    n_words int - Number of words
    wpm float 180 Reading speed, words per minute (READING_SPEED_WPM)
    messages dict[str, str] CORE_MESSAGES Messages of the errors by key, a keyword

    Legibilidad µ¶

    mu_index · Muñoz Baquedano and Muñoz Urra, 2006

    The mean number of letters per word divided by its sample variance, times 100; there are no coefficients to fit. Words without letters (numbers) are left out; with fewer than two words or without variability the index is undefined (nan).

    \[ 100 \cdot \frac{\bar{x}}{s^2}, \quad s^2 = \frac{\sum (x_i - \bar{x})^2}{n - 1} \]

    where \(x_i\) is the number of letters of a word and \(n\) the number of words.

    The index was fitted on Spanish texts, so it stays outside the get_stats of ruTS, though the object has the mu_index property. Its function is calc_mu_index of the anyTS core.

    Methods¶

    describe_grade(stat) returns the school stage and reader age for the consensus grade or the grade formula stat, consensus_grade by default; another metric raises ParameterError.

    describe_level(stat) returns the band of the interpretation scale (level_scale) of the metric stat, flesch_reading_easy by default; a metric without a scale of bands and an undefined value (nan) raise ParameterError.

    describe(stat) returns the reading of any metric by its scale: for the consensus grade and a grade formula the school stage and reader age (describe_grade), for a metric with a scale of bands (level_scale) its band, for another metric of grade_scales the stage and age of the years of schooling its scale gives, and None for a metric without a scale or with an undefined value (nan). A name that is neither in stats_desc nor a public property of the class raises UnknownStatError.

    reading_time_by_speed(wpm) returns the reading time of the text in minutes at the speed wpm, words per minute; a speed that is not a positive number raises ParameterError.

    reading_time_by_norm(norm) returns the reading times of the text in minutes at the speeds of the norm norm of reading_speed_norms, in their order; an unknown norm raises ParameterError.

    In ruTS the norms come from the ruts.constants.READING_SPEED_NORMS table, and the method returns the reading times at the upper and at the lower bound of the norm.

    sis_grade_by_stage(stage) returns the value of the Solovyev, Ivanov and Solnyshkina formula with the coefficients of the education stage stage: 2-4, 5-7 or 8-11; an unknown stage raises ParameterError.

    sis_grade_by_freq(mean_ipm, stage=None) returns the value of the formula with frequency at the mean frequency of content words mean_ipm - the mean_ipm_content attribute of LexicalStats - with the coefficients of the stage stage or, without it, of the general formula. A negative or infinite frequency, a frequency that is not a number and an unknown stage raise ParameterError, and nan gives nan.

    get_stats() returns a dictionary of the readability metrics of stats_desc.

    print_stats() prints a table of the readability metrics with their descriptions from stats_desc under the headers of stats_headers.

rs.describe("rix"), rs.describe("matskovsky_index")
rs.reading_time_by_speed(120), rs.reading_time_by_norm("grade_1")
rs.get_stats()

    Scales¶

    The describe_level method reads the Flesch index, LIX and RIX by the scales of bands of their cards (ruts.constants.READABILITY_LEVEL_SCALES), and describe reads any metric: these three by the same scales, the grade formulas and the consensus grade by the education stages of grade_to_age; for a metric without a scale it gives None.

    School stage and age¶

    grade_to_age(grade, levels=((1, 3, '1-3-й класс', '6-8 лет'), (4, 6, '4-6-й класс', '9-11 лет'), (7, 9, '7-9-й класс', '12-14 лет'), (10, 11, '10-11-й класс', '15-16 лет'), (12, 14, '1-3-й курс вуза', '17-19 лет'), (15, 17, '4-6-й курс вуза', '20-22 года')), above=('аспирантура', 'старше 22 лет'))

    Stage norms¶

    The consensus grade tells which stage a text is for, but not what a text of that stage is like. For this the ruts.constants.LEVEL_NORMS table holds the norms of the features of the stages: the usual ranges (quartiles) of the sentence length in words, the word length in letters, the share of words of five or more syllables and the lexical diversity MTLD of texts of at least 100 words labeled with the stage in the SovChLit and TextsByGrade datasets. School stages with at least 10 such texts have norms:

    Stage Texts Words per sentence Letters per word Share of words of 5+ syllables MTLD
    grades 1-3 73 8.1-10.7 4.56-5.03 0.0-0.022 104-178
    grades 4-6 12 10.2-14.6 5.16-5.33 0.039-0.052 220-373
    grades 10-11 18 15.6-17.9 5.11-5.57 0.035-0.065 277-513

    The norms of grades 1-3 come almost entirely from texts of grade 1 (70 of 73), so a text for grades 2-3 can come out above the norm, the sentence length above all. Grades 7-9 have seven texts in the datasets, and the university labels of TextsByGrade mark a Wikipedia article, a newspaper article and regulatory documents, not texts written for students of a given year, so these stages have no norms. The features of a text shorter than 100 words, MTLD above all, should not be compared with the norms.

    Features of a text¶

    level_features(source)

    The features of a text that the stage norms cover: words per sentence, letters per word (of the words the grade formulas count), the share of words of five or more syllables (the complex words of SMOG, Gunning fog and Dale-Chall) and the lexical diversity MTLD. They are computed the same way as for the norms, so they can be compared with them; the features of a Doc are computed from its text, not from the sentence boundaries of spaCy. A source that is neither a string nor a Doc raises SourceTypeError, a source without words or sentences SourceError.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    source str / Doc - Data source (a string or a Doc object)

    level_norms(grade)
