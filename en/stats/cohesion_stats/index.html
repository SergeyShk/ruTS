    Cohesion statistics¶

    CohesionStats(source, sents_extractor=None, words_extractor=None, connectors=None)

    The cohesion statistics of a text in the manner of Coh-Metrix and TAACO: the overlap of the sentences, the givenness, the temporal cohesion and the connectors of a dictionary. The sentences are compared by lemmas. The data source is a text or a Doc object of spaCy.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a source that is neither a string nor a Doc, an extractor of another type, connectors that are not a dictionary of phrases and pairs of strings;
    • SourceError - a source without words;
    • ParameterError - an unknown class or kind of a connector.

    The class extends the CohesionStats of the anyTS core with the Russian layer and, unlike the core, reads a string too. A string and a Doc without sentence boundaries are split into sentences by the Russian SentsExtractor; the words of a string come from the Russian WordsExtractor or from the words_extractor given. For a Doc with part-of-speech annotation the part of speech, the tense and the aspect come from token.pos_ and token.morph, and the lemma from the pymorphy3 analysis with the part of speech of the token (unit_info); for a string, a Doc without annotation and a Doc with a word extractor given, from the first pymorphy3 analysis (words_info), where the answer «нет» is a particle. The parts of a hyphenated word that spaCy splits are joined (во-первых is one word). The connectors are found by RussianConnectorMatcher over the dictionary of load_connectors. The print_stats table and the error messages are in Russian (COHESION_STATS_DESC and CORE_MESSAGES of ruts.constants).

    The words are classified by the pymorphy3 tags or by the Universal Dependencies annotation of a Doc:

    Notion pymorphy3 Universal Dependencies (Doc)
    Nouns NOUN NOUN, PROPN
    Pronouns NPRO, Apro, lemmas of DEMONSTRATIVE_LEMMAS PRON, DET, lemmas of DEMONSTRATIVE_LEMMAS
    Arguments NOUN, NPRO NOUN, PROPN, PRON
    Content words CONTENT_POS without STOPWORD_GRAMMEMES and demonstratives CONTENT_UD_POS without demonstratives
    Demonstratives lemmas of DEMONSTRATIVE_LEMMAS: этот, это, тот, такой, таковой, столько, сей, оный the same lemmas
    Tense and aspect grammemes past, pres, futr and perf, impf of verb forms, the tense of the existential нет and нету features Tense and Aspect 
    from ruts import CohesionStats

text = "Кот сидел на окне. Он смотрел на птиц. Птицы улетели, и кот уснул. Завтра он снова будет сидеть на этом окне."
cs = CohesionStats(text)
cs.print_stats()

    Metrics¶

    Every metric is an attribute of the object, computed when it is created - the overlaps with the next sentences and paragraphs on first use; get_stats gives those of stats_desc, all but mood_repetition by default. The functions of the cards take a sentence as the collection of its elements - lemmas of nouns, arguments or content words, values of a feature of its verbs - and see no language.

    Metric Measures Range Higher means
    noun_overlap_adjacent Share of the adjacent pairs of sentences sharing a noun 0-1, nan for one sentence Neighbouring sentences tied tighter
    noun_overlap_all Share of all the pairs of sentences sharing a noun 0-1, nan for one sentence The whole text tied tighter
    argument_overlap_adjacent Share of the adjacent pairs sharing a noun or a pronoun 0-1, nan for one sentence Neighbouring sentences tied tighter
    argument_overlap_all Share of all the pairs sharing a noun or a pronoun 0-1, nan for one sentence The whole text tied tighter
    content_overlap_adjacent Share of the adjacent pairs sharing a content word 0-1, nan for one sentence Neighbouring sentences tied tighter
    content_overlap_all Share of all the pairs sharing a content word 0-1, nan for one sentence The whole text tied tighter
    content_overlap_prop_adjacent Mean share of the shared content words (Dice) in the adjacent pairs 0-1, nan for one sentence More words shared by neighbours
    content_overlap_prop_all Mean share of the shared content words (Dice) in all the pairs 0-1, nan for one sentence More words shared across the text
    noun_overlap_2_sent Share of the nouns of a sentence repeated in one of the next two 0-1, nan for fewer than three sentences or without nouns in the compared ones More nouns carried on
    argument_overlap_2_sent Share of the arguments of a sentence repeated in one of the next two 0-1, nan for fewer than three sentences or without arguments in the compared ones More arguments carried on
    content_overlap_2_sent Share of the content words of a sentence repeated in one of the next two 0-1, nan for fewer than three sentences or without content words in the compared ones More content words carried on
    noun_overlap_para Share of the nouns of a paragraph repeated in the next one 0-1, nan for one paragraph or without nouns in the compared ones More nouns carried to the next paragraph
    argument_overlap_para Share of the arguments of a paragraph repeated in the next one 0-1, nan for one paragraph or without arguments in the compared ones More arguments carried to the next paragraph
    content_overlap_para Share of the content words of a paragraph repeated in the next one 0-1, nan for one paragraph or without content words in the compared ones More content words carried to the next paragraph
    p_pronouns Share of the pronouns among the words 0-1 More reference by pronouns
    pronoun_noun_ratio Ratio of the pronouns to the nouns 0 and above, nan without nouns More pronouns per noun
    p_demonstratives Share of the demonstratives among the words 0-1 More pointing back
    p_given Share of the content words whose lemma was used before in the text 0-1, nan without content words More given information
    tense_repetition Share of the adjacent pairs of sentences with the same dominant tense 0-1, nan without two adjacent sentences with the feature A steadier tense
    mood_repetition Share of the adjacent pairs with the same dominant mood 0-1, nan without two adjacent sentences with the feature A steadier mood
    aspect_repetition Share of the adjacent pairs with the same dominant aspect 0-1, nan without two adjacent sentences with the feature A steadier aspect
    temporal_cohesion Mean of the repetitions of temporal_features 0-1, nan when one of them is nan A steadier time frame
    connectors Connectors per 1000 words 0-1000 More explicit links
    connectors_inter Inter-sentential connectors per 1000 words 0-1000 More links to the sentences before
    connectors_intra Intra-sentential connectors per 1000 words 0-1000 More links inside the sentences
    connectors_<class>, connectors_<kind> Connectors of a class or a kind of the matcher per 1000 words 0-1000 More links of the class or the kind

    Binary overlap¶

    noun_overlap_adjacent · noun_overlap_all · argument_overlap_adjacent · argument_overlap_all · content_overlap_adjacent · content_overlap_all · Coh-Metrix

    calc_overlap(sets, adjacent=True)

    The binary overlap of Coh-Metrix (CRFNO1, CRFAO1, CRFSO1 over the adjacent pairs, CRFNOa, CRFAOa, CRFSOa over all of them): the share of the pairs of sentences that share at least one element; nan for a text shorter than two sentences.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of pairs sharing an element}}{\textrm{Number of pairs}} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    sets list[set[str]] - Elements of every sentence
    adjacent bool True Count the adjacent pairs only, otherwise all the pairs

    Proportional overlap¶

    content_overlap_prop_adjacent · content_overlap_prop_all · Coh-Metrix

    calc_proportional_overlap(sets, adjacent=True)

    The proportional overlap of Coh-Metrix (CRFCWO1, CRFCWOa): the Dice coefficient of a pair of sentences (dice) averaged over the pairs; nan for a text shorter than two sentences.

    \[ \frac{1}{\textrm{Number of pairs}} \sum_{(A, B)} \textrm{Dice}(A, B) \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    sets list[set[str]] - Elements of every sentence
    adjacent bool True Count the adjacent pairs only, otherwise all the pairs 
    dice(first, second)

    The Dice coefficient of two sets, 0 for two empty sets.

    \[ \frac{2 \times |A \cap B|}{|A| + |B|} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    first frozenset[str] - First set
    second frozenset[str] - Second set

    Overlap with the next sentences and paragraphs¶

    noun_overlap_2_sent · argument_overlap_2_sent · content_overlap_2_sent · noun_overlap_para · argument_overlap_para · content_overlap_para · TAACO

    The adjacent overlap of TAACO, by the lemmas of the nouns, of the arguments and of the content words: the share of the distinct lemmas of a sentence repeated in one of the next two sentences (adjacent_overlap_2_*_sent), and of a paragraph repeated in the next paragraph (adjacent_overlap_*_para), over all the sentences or paragraphs that have them (calc_forward_overlap). Unlike the binary overlap, a pair of segments counts by the share of the lemmas they share. A paragraph is a part of the text between blank lines that holds words; a word that carries paragraph_start opens one, inside a sentence too, as when a parser joins a heading to the next sentence. TAACO takes every line for a paragraph: a text with a paragraph on every line and no blank lines is one paragraph here, and its *_para are nan.

    calc_forward_overlap(sets, span=1)

    The share of the elements of a segment repeated in the next ones: the distinct elements of every segment with span segments after it that one of these segments holds, over the distinct elements of those segments; nan for no more segments than span or for segments without elements.

    \[ \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n-k} |A_i \cap (A_{i+1} \cup \dots \cup A_{i+k})|}{\sum_{i=1}^{n-k} |A_i|} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    sets list[set[str]] - Elements of every segment
    span int 1 Number \(k\) of the next segments a segment is compared with

    Segments that are a string, a Doc, an iterator or a segment that is not a list of strings raise SourceTypeError, a span that is not an integer above 0 ParameterError.

    In ruTS both a string and a Doc are split into paragraphs.

    from ruts.cohesion_stats import calc_forward_overlap

calc_forward_overlap([{"кот", "окно"}, {"птица"}, {"кот"}], span=2)
text = "Кот сидел на окне. Он смотрел на птиц.\n\nПтицы улетели, и кот уснул. Завтра он снова будет сидеть на этом окне."
paragraphs = CohesionStats(text)
paragraphs.n_paragraphs, paragraphs.noun_overlap_para, paragraphs.content_overlap_para

    Pronouns and demonstratives¶

    p_pronouns · pronoun_noun_ratio · p_demonstratives

    The pronouns, the nouns and the demonstratives are the words analyze marks so, with the features of word_info by default.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of pronouns}}{\textrm{Number of words}}, \quad \frac{\textrm{Number of pronouns}}{\textrm{Number of nouns}}, \quad \frac{\textrm{Number of demonstratives}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]

    In ruTS they are marked by the functions of the word features after the table of the classes of words.

    Givenness¶

    p_given

    count_given(sents)

    The number of given elements: an element is given when the same lemma was used earlier in any sentence, the current one included; the first occurrence is new.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    sents list[list[str]] - Lemmas of every sentence in the order of the text

    Temporal cohesion¶

    tense_repetition · mood_repetition · aspect_repetition · temporal_cohesion · Coh-Metrix

    calc_repetition(sents)

    The repetition of the tense and of the aspect of Coh-Metrix (SMTEMP): the share of the adjacent pairs of sentences whose verbs have the same dominant value of the feature; a pair where one of the sentences has no verb with the feature is skipped, and without a single such pair the value is nan.

    \[ \frac{\textrm{Number of pairs with the same dominant value}}{\textrm{Number of pairs with the feature}} \]
    Parameter Type Default Description
    sents list[list[str]] - Values of the feature of the verbs of every sentence 
    dominant(values)

    The dominant value: the most frequent one, the first of the equally frequent; None for an empty list.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    values list[str] - Values

    CohesionStats of ruTS does not compare the mood: mood_repetition is always nan, and temporal_cohesion is the mean of tense_repetition and aspect_repetition.

    Connectors¶

    connectors · connectors_inter · connectors_intra · connectors_<class> · connectors_<kind>

    The occurrences of the connectors found in every sentence by the matcher of connector_matcher_class, per 1000 words. An inter-sentential connector links a sentence to the ones before: it opens a sentence, unless the matcher takes it for a subordinator (is_subordinator), whose clause or phrase belongs to the same sentence; the others are intra-sentential. Every class and kind of the matcher gives a density: connectors_causal, connectors_adversative, connectors_concessive, connectors_temporal, connectors_additive, connectors_conditional, connectors_reformulative, connectors_primary and connectors_secondary by default.

    \[ 1000 \cdot \frac{\textrm{Number of connectors}}{\textrm{Number of words}} \]
    load_connectors()

    The connector dictionary of ruTS: the file ruts/resources/connectors.tsv, 316 connectors with a class of CONNECTOR_CLASSES and a kind of CONNECTOR_TYPES of ruts.constants, compiled after the classification of Krioni, Nikin and Filippova (2008) and checked against the Ru-RSTreebank marker list, the Ruscon database and the causal marker list of Toldova et al. (2018). The one-word «и», «а», «но» are in the dictionary, as in Coh-Metrix and TAACO; «что» and «как» are no connectors.

    Class Key Examples
    Causal (consequential and purposive included) causal потому что, поэтому, в результате, чтобы, таким образом
    Adversative adversative но, однако, зато, с другой стороны, в то же время
    Concessive concessive хотя, несмотря на, тем не менее, и всё же, в любом случае
    Temporal temporal когда, затем, после того как, тем временем, в дальнейшем
    Additive additive и, также, кроме того, более того, например, во-первых
    Conditional conditional если, в случае если, при условии что, в противном случае
    Reformulative reformulative то есть, иными словами, в общем, подводя итог

    The primary connectors (primary) are conjunctions, adverbs and particles (потому что, однако, затем), the secondary ones (secondary) lexicalized phrases with nouns and verbs (в результате этого, с другой стороны, иными словами). The rules by which a word of the dictionary counts as a connector are those of the matcher.

    A list of sentences that is a string, a Doc, an iterator, a set or a table raises SourceTypeError, and so does a sentence that is not a list of strings (check_words), such as a list of spaCy tokens. The overlaps (calc_overlap, calc_proportional_overlap, calc_overlaps) take a sentence as a set too; count_given and calc_repetition count the words in order and refuse a set.

    from ruts.cohesion_stats import calc_overlap, calc_repetition, count_given

sets = [{"кот", "окно"}, {"птица"}, {"птица", "кот"}, {"окно"}]
calc_overlap(sets), calc_overlap(sets, adjacent=False)
count_given([["кот", "окно"], ["птица"], ["птица", "кот"]])
calc_repetition([["past"], ["past", "pres", "past"], ["futr"]])

    Overlaps at once¶

    calc_overlaps(sets, proportional=True)

    The values of calc_overlap and calc_proportional_overlap over the adjacent and over all the pairs at once, as an Overlap named tuple with the fields adjacent, all, prop_adjacent and prop_all; without proportional the last two are nan.     Connector matcher¶

    RussianConnectorMatcher(connectors)

    The search of the connectors (discourse markers: because, however, on the other hand) of a dictionary in the sentences of a text. The dictionary gives the class of every connector - causal, adversative, concessive, temporal, additive, conditional or reformulative (CONNECTOR_CLASSES) - and its kind, primary or secondary (CONNECTOR_KINDS). A connector is matched by the normalized forms of its words, the longest one at every position, without overlaps; a period or a hyphen inside a connector may be split off by the tokenizer (e.g. as e, g). An occurrence is a Connector: the number of the sentence sent, the positions start and end of its words, the connector text as the dictionary writes it, its class cls and kind kind.

    Raises:

    • SourceTypeError - a dictionary that is not a mapping of a phrase to a pair of strings;
    • ParameterError - an unknown class or kind.

    RussianConnectorMatcher is a subclass of the core ConnectorMatcher with the rules of Russian. Words and connectors are compared in lower case without the letter ё and stress marks (normalize_yo). A one-word connector takes a part of speech of the core CONNECTOR_POS (CCONJ, SCONJ, PART, ADV, ADP, INTJ, X) or of CONNECTOR_POS_EXTRA for its form (for example: словом - NOUN, главное - ADJ, NOUN, допустим - VERB, точнее - ADJ, т.е. - PUNCT, значит - VERB, ежели and коли - NOUN, PROPN, VERB): «раз» as a noun is no connector. The context rules are lists of ruts.constants: words after a connector (CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_AFTER: пока что, хоть бы) and before it (CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_BEFORE: ещё раз, и так далее) that make it no connector; connectors that count inside a sentence only after particular words (CONNECTOR_ONLY_AFTER: и отсюда) and do not count at its start (CONNECTOR_NOT_AT_START: the interrogative отчего); parts of speech of the word after a connector that make it a preposition or a member of the clause (CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_AFTER_POS: после обеда, едва заметный). The rules read through a punctuation mark only for CONNECTOR_READ_THROUGH_MARKS (не знал, отчего), so the parenthetical значит, stays a connector, and a mark between the words of a connector of CONNECTOR_SPLIT_BY_MARKS splits it (так, как). These parts of speech and lists are attributes of the matcher: CONNECTOR_POS is pos_tags, the others are named without CONNECTOR_ in lower case (CONNECTOR_BLOCKED_AFTER is blocked_after); the attributes not_after_pos and start_only_pos are empty in ruTS, so these rules of find do not apply.

    find(words, sent_index, pos, breaks, blocked) returns the occurrences of the connectors in a sentence in the order of its words. The words are those of one sentence without punctuation, the first one its start. With the parts of speech, a one-word connector counts only with one of pos_tags or of pos_extra for its form, never after one of not_after_pos, and with one of start_only_pos only at the start of the sentence; a word without a part of speech (None) passes. Without the parts of speech the rules that read them are skipped. The positions of the punctuation marks breaks cut a connector off its neighbours: a rule does not read a word across a mark, so in so, the end the word after the comma neither blocks nor lets the connector count, unless the connector is one of read_through_marks, and a mark between the words of a connector of split_by_marks makes it none; without them the words are read as adjacent. No connector starts at a word of blocked, which a language rules out by more than the words and their parts of speech, such as its parse.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    words list[str] - Words of the sentence
    sent_index int 0 Number of the sentence, written into the occurrences
    pos list[str] None Parts of speech of the words
    breaks set[int] None Positions of the words a punctuation mark stands before
    blocked set[int] None Positions of the words no connector starts at

    Words that are not a list of strings and parts of speech that are not a list of strings and None (the tags token.pos_ of spaCy, not the numbers token.pos) and positions that are not a collection raise SourceTypeError, a number of the sentence or a position that is not an integer, a position of a mark that is not that of a word after the first, a blocked position that is not that of a word and parts of speech not as many as the words ParameterError.

    is_subordinator(connector, pos=None) checks whether an occurrence of a connector ties its clause or phrase to its own sentence: a connector of subordinators, or a one-word connector whose part of speech pos is SCONJ or ADP, but none of parcellation. The clause of a subordinating conjunction and the phrase of a preposition belong to the sentence of the connector wherever it stands, so a subordinator opening a sentence (when he came, ..., despite the rain, ...) does not link it to the ones before. A connector that is not a Connector raises SourceTypeError.

    The subordinators of ruTS besides the one-word ones tagged SCONJ and ADP are CONNECTOR_SUBORDINATORS: the multi-word conjunctions and prepositions (так как, после того как, несмотря на) and the one-word ones the spaCy models or pymorphy3 tag otherwise (ежели, коли, пока, раз, хоть, едва). A conjunction of CONNECTOR_PARCELLATION (ибо) opening a sentence attaches it to the one before, so it is no subordinator.

    Word features¶

    word_info(word)

    words_info(sents, text)

    token_info(token)

    ud_info(word, pos, tense, aspect)

    unit_info(unit)

    The features of a word for the cohesion statistics, a WordInfo named tuple: lemma, noun, pronoun, demonstrative, argument, content, tense and aspect after the table of the classes of words. word_info takes them from the first pymorphy3 analysis; words_info does the same for the words of the sentences of a text, where the answer «нет» (Нет, не видел) is a particle without tense. token_info takes them from the spaCy annotation (token.pos_, Tense, Aspect), ud_info from a given part of speech, tense and aspect of UD; the lemma comes from the pymorphy3 analysis with that part of speech (ruts.utils.lemmatize), not from token.lemma_. unit_info gives the features of a word from its tokens: a single token by token_info, a hyphenated word of several tokens (жили-были, назовите-ка) by the pymorphy3 analysis of the joined text mapped to UD (word_to_ud).

    Parameter Type Default Description
    word str - Word (word_info, ud_info)
    sents list[list[str]] - Words of the sentences of a text (words_info)
    text str - Text the words are extracted from (words_info)
    token Token - spaCy token (token_info)
    pos, tense, aspect str - Part of speech, tense and aspect of UD (ud_info)
    unit list[Token] - Tokens of a word (unit_info) 
    from ruts.cohesion_stats import word_info

word_info("смотрел")

    Words of a Doc¶

    split_doc_units(source, sents_extractor, join_hyphens=False)

    Splits the words of a Doc or a Span into the sentences of a sentence extractor, for a Doc without sentence boundaries or one to split otherwise: the sentences are found in its text in their order, and a word of iter_doc_units belongs to the sentence its first token lies in. The words outside the sentences found, those the extractor drops, are left out, so no sentence found gives no words.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    source Doc / Span - Doc or Span object
    sents_extractor SentsExtractor - Sentence extraction tool
    join_hyphens bool False Join the parts of hyphenated words

    CohesionStats always joins the parts of hyphenated words, the function only with join_hyphens=True.

    unit_text(unit)

    unit_pos(unit)

    unit_text gives the text of a word from its tokens without a byte order mark, stress marks and soft hyphens (ruts.utils.strip_marks), unit_pos its part of speech of UD: that of the token for a single token, and that of connector_pos of the joined text for a hyphenated word of several tokens.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    unit list[Token] - Tokens of a word 
    import spacy
from ruts import SentsExtractor
from ruts.cohesion_stats import split_doc_units, unit_text

doc = spacy.blank("ru")("Жили-были дед да баба. Во-первых, кот спит.")
units = split_doc_units(doc, SentsExtractor())
[[unit_text(unit) for unit in sent] for sent in units]
units = split_doc_units(doc, SentsExtractor(), join_hyphens=True)
[[unit_text(unit) for unit in sent] for sent in units]

[['Жили-были', 'дед', 'да', 'баба'], ['Во-первых', 'кот', 'спит']]
