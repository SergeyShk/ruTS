    Statistic functions¶

    All functions work with spaCy Token objects: the calc_* functions take a sequence of tokens (Doc, Span or a list), the is_* and count_* functions - a single token. The functions of the dependency tree come from the anyTS core, the functions of the Russian constructions are those of ruTS. Syntactic relations and morphological features are taken from the Universal Dependencies annotation; relation subtypes (acl:relcl, nsubj:pass, nummod:gov) are counted by the base relation where noted.

    The feature definitions follow Ivanov, Solnyshkina and Solovyev (2018); the original feature names and their correlation coefficients with the textbook grade from that paper are given in parentheses.

    Hyphenated words¶

    A tokenizer may split a word at its hyphens (well-known into well, -, known), and a parser then hangs every part on its own head. With join_hyphens=True the functions of the tree take a word whose parts anyts.utils.joins_previous joins for one word with its hyphens. A word holding the head of the sentence has no head; the head of another one is that of its part hanging outside it, the nearest to the root (a word rather than a hyphen when equal). The dependents of a word are the words whose head hangs on one of its tokens, each given by that part (or by its first part when it hangs by a hyphen); its subtree is the word and the words that hang on it, directly or through punctuation. So every word has one dependency and is the dependent of one word.

    SyntaxStats and the functions of the Russian constructions always join hyphenated words (во-первых is one word). The functions of the core, is_predicate, count_noun_modifiers, is_passive, has_agent and is_agentless included, take join_hyphens=False by default: they give the values of SyntaxStats with join_hyphens=True.

    Dependency distances¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.calc_dependency_distances()

    The dependency distances (Liu 2008): the distance between a word and its head in positions of words, punctuation left out, in the order of the words. The head of a sentence and a word whose head lies outside the sequence are skipped.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc/Span/list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one position

    SyntaxStats derives from the distances the mean (mean_dependency_distance), the standard deviation over all dependencies of the text (std_dependency_distance), the maximum in a sentence averaged over sentences with dependencies (max_dependency_distance), and the share of adjacent relations of distance 1 (p_adjacent_dependencies); one-word sentences enter none of the statistics. The mean dependency distance grows with sentence length; the maximum distance is the best of the tree features at the level of an individual sentence.

    Example

    import spacy
from ruts.syntax_stats import calc_dependency_distances

nlp = spacy.load("ru_core_news_sm")
calc_dependency_distances(
    nlp("Повышение эффективности использования ресурсов предприятия обсуждалось на совещании.")
)
# [5, 1, 1, 1, 1, 1, 2]

    Tree depth¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.calc_tree_depth()

    The depth of the dependency tree: the longest path from the head of a sentence to a leaf, in relations between words; the maximum for several sentences, 0 for a sentence of one word. With join_hyphens a hyphenated word is one word, and the words that hang on any of its parts are one level below it.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc/Span/list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    The depth is LONGEST_PATH of the paper (r = 0.84); SyntaxStats averages it over sentences (tree_depth).

    Leaves, subtrees and branching¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.count_children()

    count_children(token, join_hyphens=False) counts the dependent words of a token.

    A word without dependents is a leaf (LEAVES_NUMBER, r = 0.84), a word with dependents heads a subtree (PATH_NUMBER, r = 0.87). SyntaxStats counts the numbers of leaves and subtrees per sentence (leaves_per_sent, subtrees_per_sent), the ratio of words to leaves (AVERAGE_PATH, r = 0.84; nodes_per_leaf) and the distribution of words by number of dependents - the branching profile (c_children).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    Valency¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.calc_valency()

    The valency of a token: the number of its dependent words, the relations cc, conj, parataxis and punct left out (anyts.constants.VALENCY_IGNORED_DEPS); calc_valency(token, join_hyphens=True) counts a hyphenated dependent once, so came in Some-one came has one dependent and not two.

    SyntaxStats averages the valency over finite verb forms (VERBS_DEP, r = 0.43; verb_valency), see is_finite_verb.

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    Coordination chains¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.calc_coordination_chains()

    The lengths of the coordination chains, in the order of their first words. A chain is a group of words linked by conj, whichever word each conjunct hangs on: Universal Dependencies attaches every conjunct to the first one (pears → apples, plums → apples), ClearNLP to the previous one (pears → apples, plums → pears); both give one chain of three for apples, pears and plums. A nested coordination (cats and dogs, or birds) and an enumeration split between several heads are one chain too. The length of a chain is the number of its words; a conjunct whose head lies outside the sequence is not linked to it. With join_hyphens a hyphenated word is one conjunct, linked by the conj of any of its parts (member and vice-governor is a chain of two).

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc/Span/list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    The number of chains per sentence (SOCHIN_NUMBER, r = 0.93) and their mean length (AVERAGE_SOCHIN_LENGTH, r = 0.87) are the strongest syntactic predictors of textbook complexity.

    Clauses¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.is_clause_head(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_infinitive_clause(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_infinitive_periphrasis(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_subordinate_clause_head(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_predicate()

    Checking whether a token heads a clause. A clause is headed by the sentence root or by a word with the relation ccomp, advcl, acl, acl:relcl, csubj or csubj:pass, except full participles, adverbial participles and infinitives attached to a noun («желание уйти»): participial and adverbial participle clauses are counted separately. An infinitive attached to a verb (xcomp) heads a clause of its own («хочет уйти», «попросил его уйти», is_infinitive_clause), except in combinations of one predication (is_infinitive_periphrasis): with the modal and phasal verbs of INFINITIVE_PERIPHRASES («может уйти», «должен прийти», «начал читать», «перестал курить») and the hortative «давай», «давайте» without a negation (HORTATIVE_WORDS). A parenthetical construction (parataxis) and a coordinated predicate (a conj relation from the clause head) form their own clause only if it can be a predicate (is_predicate): «Он сказал: „Уходи“» and «думала, что он умен» are clauses, while the parentheticals «например», «конечно», «во-первых», which spaCy models also attach as parataxis, are not.

    is_predicate(token, join_hyphens=False) checks whether a word can be the predicate of its clause: a verb (VERB, AUX) or a word with a subject of its own (nsubj, csubj and their subtypes, anyts.constants.SUBJECT_DEPS), so the nominal predicate of he is tall passes and a parenthetical word does not.

    A subordinate clause is a clause with the relation ccomp, advcl, acl, acl:relcl, csubj, csubj:pass or xcomp, as well as a coordinated predicate of a subordinate clause (PODCHIN_RATE, r = 0.64). SyntaxStats counts clauses and subordinate clauses per sentence (clauses_per_sent, subordinate_clauses_per_sent), the mean clause length in words (mean_clause_len) and the share of sentences with at least one subordinate clause (PODCHIN_NUMBER, r = 0.62; p_complex_sents).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token

    Noun phrase modifiers¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.count_noun_modifiers()

    count_noun_modifiers(token, join_hyphens=False) counts the modifiers of a noun phrase: the dependents with the relations amod, det, nmod, nummod, acl and their subtypes (anyts.constants.NOUN_MODIFIER_DEPS); conj and appos are no modifiers.

    SyntaxStats averages the number of modifiers over nouns and proper names (NOUNS_DEP, r = 0.88; modifiers_per_noun).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    Genitive chains¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.calc_genitive_chains(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_genitive_modifier(), ruts.syntax_stats.calc_modifier_chains()

    Computation of genitive chain lengths - the chains of calc_modifier_chains whose modifiers are prepositionless genitive modifiers (is_genitive_modifier): a word with the nmod relation in the genitive case without a dependent preposition (case), «дом отца», but not «дом у дороги». SyntaxStats counts the number of chains per sentence (genitive_chains_per_sent) and the maximum length over the text (max_genitive_chain_len).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc/Span/list[Token] - Sequence of tokens

    Example

    import spacy
from ruts.syntax_stats import calc_genitive_chains

nlp = spacy.load("ru_core_news_sm")
calc_genitive_chains(nlp("Повышение эффективности использования ресурсов обсуждалось долго."))
# [3]

    calc_modifier_chains¶

    The lengths of the chains of nested modifiers, in the order of the words. A chain is two or more modifiers each hanging on the previous one, such as the complements of the increase of the efficiency of the use of resources (length 3); the check is_modifier of a language says what a modifier is (a complement with a preposition, a noun in the genitive). A chain starts at a modifier whose head is none, and its length is the number of modifiers in its longest branch. The links are those of get_children: punctuation is no link, and with join_hyphens a hyphenated word is one link. A check that is not callable raises SourceTypeError.

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc/Span/list[Token] - Sequence of tokens
    is_modifier Callable[[Token], bool] - Whether a word is a modifier of the chain
    join_hyphens bool False Take a hyphenated word for one word

    Participial and adverbial participle clauses¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.is_participle_clause(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_converb_clause(), ruts.syntax_stats.subtree_len()

    Checking whether a token heads a participial or adverbial participle clause: a full participle (is_participle) or an adverbial participle (is_converb) with at least one dependent word, not counting coordination and parenthetical relations. Short participles («дом построен») are predicates rather than modifiers and form no clauses. The clause length is the number of words in the subtree including the participle itself (subtree_len). SyntaxStats counts clauses per sentence (PRICH_RATE, r = 0.91; DEEPRICH_RATE, r = 0.44) and their mean length (PRICH_V, DEEPRICH_V).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token

    Passive voice¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.is_passive(), ruts.syntax_stats.has_agent(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_agentless()

    is_passive(token, join_hyphens=False) checks whether a word is a passive verb form: a verb with Voice=Pass or a dependent nsubj:pass, csubj:pass, aux:pass, or nsubjpass, csubjpass, auxpass of ClearNLP (PASSIVE_DEPS).

    has_agent(token, join_hyphens=False) checks whether a word has an agent: a dependent obl:agent, or agent of ClearNLP (AGENT_DEPS).

    is_agentless(token, join_hyphens=False) checks whether a word is a passive form (is_passive) without an agent (has_agent).

    In Russian the passive is a passive participle or the reflexive passive («обсуждалось»), and an agentless form is «дом построен», but not «дом построен рабочими». SyntaxStats counts the share of passive forms among all verb forms (p_passive) and the share of agentless forms among passive ones (p_agentless_passive).

    Verb forms¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.is_finite_verb(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_participle(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_converb(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_infinitive()

    is_finite_verb(token) checks whether a token is a finite verb: the part of speech VERB with VerbForm=Fin, so a finite auxiliary (AUX) is not one.

    is_infinitive(token) checks whether a token is an infinitive, VerbForm=Inf whatever its part of speech.

    is_participle(token) checks whether a token is a full participle (VerbForm=Part without Variant=Short), is_converb(token) - whether it is an adverbial participle (VerbForm=Conv). SyntaxStats counts infinitives per sentence (infinitives_per_sent).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token

    Negations¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.is_negation()

    Checking whether a token is a negative particle - a particle (PART) with the feature Polarity=Neg or the particle «не», «ни». The conjunction «ни» in the «ни… ни» construction does not count as a negation. SyntaxStats counts negations per sentence (NEG, r = 0.70; negations_per_sent).

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token

    Split predicates¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.find_split_predicates(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_light_verb(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_reflexive(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_split_predicate_noun()

    Search for split predicates - an officialese marker: a light verb with a lemma from LIGHT_VERBS (осуществлять, производить, проводить, обеспечивать, оказывать, принимать, иметь and their reflexive passives проводиться, приниматься, вестись) with a nominal part - a noun with a verbal lemma (ruts.utils.is_verbal_noun) or a lemma from SPLIT_PREDICATE_NOUNS (роль, работа, помощь, мера) with a verb of its own phrase (оказать помощь, принять меры, but not получить работу): осуществлять проверку, оказать помощь, принято решение, проверка проводится. At most one nominal part is taken per verb in the order of preference obj, nsubj:pass, nsubj (only for a reflexive or passive verb, is_reflexive), iobj, nmod, obl; prepositional adjuncts and the passive agent (obl:agent, instrumental obl) are not counted. The verb lemma is checked both by spaCy and by pymorphy3 (get_lemma, ruts.utils.lemmatize). SyntaxStats counts split predicates per sentence (split_predicates_per_sent) and keeps the found pairs in split_predicates; the ratio of nouns to verb forms (noun_verb_ratio) is computed from the class counters.

    Parameters:

    Parameter Type Default Description
    tokens Doc/Span/list[Token] - Sequence of tokens

    Helper functions¶

    ruts.syntax_stats.is_word(), ruts.syntax_stats.get_words(), ruts.syntax_stats.is_root(), ruts.syntax_stats.base_dep(), ruts.syntax_stats.get_children(), ruts.syntax_stats.subtree_len(), ruts.syntax_stats.has_feature(), ruts.syntax_stats.get_lemma()

    is_word¶

    Checks whether a token is a word: not whitespace and not a token of is_punctuation, so symbols like %, €, + and invisible characters are no words either. The words of iter_doc_tokens and the nodes of the dependency tree of the syntactic statistics are such tokens.

    get_words¶

    get_words(tokens, join_hyphens=False) returns the words of a sequence of tokens in their order; with join_hyphens=True a word the tokenizer split at its hyphens is one word, given by its part that holds its relation, as with the dependents of get_children (governor in He is vice-governor), or by its first part when a hyphen holds it.

    is_root¶

    Checks whether a token is the head of its sentence: its head is the token itself.

    base_dep¶

    The relation of a token without its subtype, which Universal Dependencies separates by a colon: nsubj:pass gives nsubj, expl:pass gives expl.

    get_children, subtree_len¶

    get_children(token, join_hyphens=False) returns the dependent words of a token, punctuation and whitespace left out; with join_hyphens=True, the dependents of the whole hyphenated word the token belongs to.

    subtree_len(token, join_hyphens=False) is the number of words in the subtree of a token: the token itself and all of its direct and indirect dependents.

    has_feature¶

    Checks whether a token carries a morphological feature with a given value: has_feature(token, "VerbForm", "Fin").

    Parameter Type Default Description
    token Token - Token
    field str - Name of the feature
    value str - Value of the feature

    get_lemma¶

    get_lemma(token) returns the lemma of a token in lower case from pymorphy3 by the part of speech of the token.
